Who We Are

A Cork agency, built on the water's edge

Quigg Property was founded on a simple belief: that buying, selling or letting a home should be guided by honesty, deep local knowledge and a genuine commitment to results. Based in Cork, our roots run along the harbour — from Cobh to Crosshaven, Midleton to Kinsale — where we've helped hundreds of families and investors find their place.

As part of the Keller Williams Global Reach network, we combine that hyper-local expertise with the tools, marketing reach and international referral network of the world's largest real estate franchise — giving our clients the very best of both worlds.
